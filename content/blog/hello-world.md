## How posts work

Every `.md` file in `content/blog/` becomes a post at `/blog/<filename>`. The top block (between the `---` lines) holds the **title**, **date** and **summary**.

- Use normal Markdown: headings, lists, links, `inline code`
- Add a file, commit, deploy: that's publishing
